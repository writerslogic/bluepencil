# Chapter Three: Departure

They drove north along the coast road until the pavement gave out. Ames talked the whole way. He talked about the cannery and the storm of 1987 and the families who had left.

Mara listened. She watched the water. She counted the fence posts.

At the end of the road a white tower stood against the sky, exactly where no map had put it.

"Well," Ames said softly, "that's new."
