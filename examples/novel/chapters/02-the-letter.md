# Chapter Two: The Letter

Morning came gray and slow. Mara read the letter again at the small table by the window, the letter her sister had mailed three weeks before she disappeared.

*Come to Harrow Creek,* it said. *Ask for the keeper of the lighthouse.*

There was no lighthouse in Harrow Creek. Mara had checked. She had checked the maps, and she had checked the county records, and she had checked the old newspapers at the library in the city.

Suddenly someone knocked. She opened the door. She saw Dr. Ames standing in the rain with two cups of coffee.

"I thought you might want this," he said. "And I thought you might want to know that your sister stayed in this same room."
