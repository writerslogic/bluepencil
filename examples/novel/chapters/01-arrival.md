# Chapter One: Arrival

The bus left Mara at the edge of town just after dusk. She felt the cold settle into her coat. She saw the diner lights across the road. She noticed a man watching her from the doorway.

"You lost?" he asked.

"Not yet," Mara said. She really didn't know why she said it. The words just came out, quickly and quietly, before she could stop them.

He laughed. "Mr. Hale runs the motel. Tell him Dr. Ames sent you."

## The Motel

The motel sat low against the hill like an animal waiting out the weather. Mara walked slowly up the gravel drive, and the gravel crunched under her boots with every step she took toward the office door.

Her heart skipped a beat when the door opened. The old man inside looked at her for a moment, then looked at the clock, then looked at her again.

"Room four," he said. "The heater works if you kick it."
